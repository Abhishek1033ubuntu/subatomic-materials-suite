## [v1.4.0] - 2026-11-27

### Added
* **Module 07 (`07_mmwave_waveguide_boundary_optimizer`):** Added millimeter-wave waveguide boundary-condition and Passive Intermodulation (PIM) solver (`waveguide_boundary_optimizer.py`) targeting hollow rectangular waveguides above 30 GHz.
* **Graded Boundary Stack Screening:** Implemented cascaded transmission-line surface impedance modeling for the optimal diamagnetic Cu / TaN / Ag-core (1.8 &mu;m) / ultrathin-Au passivation (40 nm) wall coating.
* **Roughness & PIM Solvers:** Added Huray snowball surface-roughness attenuation modeling and phenomenological IM<sub>3</sub> estimation accounting for ferromagnetic trace impurities and metal-oxide-metal (MOM) flange tunneling barriers.
